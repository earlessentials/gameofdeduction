# DEDUCE — Content and continuity review

18 original fictional investigations in three volumes. Each volume has two easy, two medium and two complicated cases. Totals: 276 inspectable records, 126 scene locations, 300 four-option checkpoints (including instrument comparisons and final theories), and 12 case-specific instruments added for Volumes II–III.

## Case model and review limits

Canonical solutions are authored before their evidence. Source dependencies, case IDs and answer keys stay server-side. Volume I content and IDs remain unchanged. New tools require their referenced evidence and all four instrument views before leaving the assigned stage. Interview confrontation branches require the linked source record. All Indonesian localized content is checked for the forbidden standalone pronoun; use kamu.

The mysteries are fiction. Medical, fire-response and forensic details have not received professional specialist review. Case 007 and case 015 explicitly flag relevant details for validation. Case 016 prominently defines invented duplication rules before its deductions and makes no real-world claim that duplicated biological material reproduces fingerprints or personal identity. Case 018 uses open-question interviews, independently checked records, adult consent and acknowledged uncertainty; it does not use recovered-memory techniques.

## Background checks used during authoring

- NICE, Epilepsies in children, young people and adults, diagnosis and assessment: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng217/chapter/diagnosis-and-assessment-of-epilepsy — normal routine EEG is not an exclusion of epilepsy; clinical context and further assessment matter.
- National Academies, Identifying the Culprit, basic research chapter: https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/18891/chapter/6 — perception and memory limits inform the fictional eyewitness case.
- National Academies, applied eyewitness identification research: https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/18891/chapter/7 — later information exposure can affect recollection and confidence.

These sources inform general principles only. All numerical findings, case records, timelines and characters in DEDUCE are invented; the game does not claim clinical, legal or emergency-response certification.
